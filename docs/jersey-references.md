# Jersey references — archived season research

The current wardrobe uses signature team colourways rather than season replicas, following the user’s revised direction. T1 is scarlet/black, GAM black/yellow, Gen.G black/gold, HLE all-black with double orange V-neck and cuff piping, DK mint/black, G2 black/white and Fnatic black/orange. The photos below document the previous 2026 research and no longer describe the shipped designs. Season labels have been removed from the picker.

Reviewed 2026-10-17. SVG garments adapt the visible cut, colour blocks and fabric print to Gumi's proportions. Team crests, sponsors, wordmarks and manufacturer marks are omitted. These are adaptations, not official products. Product photos are reference material only and are not bundled into the app.
